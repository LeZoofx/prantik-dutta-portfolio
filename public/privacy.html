Privacy & accessibility.

This is Prantik Dutta's professional portfolio, based in Mumbai, India. For a privacy request, correction, accessibility issue or content concern, email prantikdutta1611@gmail.com. Include the relevant page or project link.

Browsing the portfolio.

The portfolio does not run its own advertising pixels, visitor analytics, accounts or enquiry forms. Its fonts, thumbnails and selected document visuals are hosted with the site. Performance choices use browser-provided device, reduced-motion and connection signals locally; the portfolio does not send those signals to an analytics service.

GitHub Pages serves the site and processes connection information, including IP addresses and request details, for hosting and security. GitHub controls those records and their retention. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Video embeds are optional.

No YouTube player/API or Instagram iframe is loaded until you allow video embeds. The notice appears when you request playback. Keeping thumbnails leaves the work, process notes and original-source links available.

Allowing embeds connects your browser to Google/YouTube or Meta/Instagram. These providers receive connection information such as your IP address, browser details and the embedding page's origin. They may use cookies, device storage and account information under their own policies. Data may be processed outside your country.

  • YouTube uses its privacy-enhanced embed domain. This limits some personalization; it does not mean the player collects no data. Google privacy · YouTube terms.
  • Instagram controls its embed, account requirements and playback availability. Meta/Instagram privacy.

Your consent is held in this tab's memory, without a portfolio cookie or saved identifier. It ends on reload. Use Contact → Reset video choice to withdraw it: loaded players are removed and automatic previews turn off. Withdrawal does not erase data already received by a provider or delete provider cookies; use their privacy controls and your browser's site-data settings for that.

External source, LinkedIn and WhatsApp links open those services only when selected. Their policies apply there.

If you contact me.

Email and messaging providers handle the connection. I receive the contact details and project information you choose to send and use them to respond, discuss the work and handle any agreed engagement. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for the enquiry.

Correspondence is kept while the enquiry or resulting work needs it, and where legal record-keeping requires it. You can request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where it is the basis for processing, or raise a grievance using the email above. Applicable rights and any lawful retention requirements depend on your location and the records involved. GitHub, Google and Meta handle requests concerning data they control through their own channels.

The portfolio does not sell visitor contact data or run targeted advertising. Third-party player processing remains subject to the providers' policies and your choices.

Motion, navigation & captions.

The site respects your device's reduced-motion setting. Contact → Pause motion pauses ongoing site animations and uses the existing reduced-motion behaviour. Client, results and process scrolling have pause controls; frame rotation and previews have their own controls. You can use Overview or the searchable Work index, a keyboard, and the skip-to-content link.

Project notes use headings, native expandable sections and source buttons. Their images are loaded only as needed. Dialogs support Escape and return focus when closed. YouTube full players request captions where the source provides them; caption and transcript availability is controlled by the original publisher.

This is a description of the current features, not a claim of independently certified WCAG conformance. If something prevents you accessing a project, send the link and the format you need.
